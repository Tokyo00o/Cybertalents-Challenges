# Reward

> **Challenge:** When I discover a vulnerability, I may receive a reward or be mentioned on a wall of fame. What kind of program am I participating in?
>
> **Flag format:** No spaces.

---

## Solution

Some organizations run programs that recognize security researchers for responsibly reporting vulnerabilities. Recognition may come in the form of a monetary reward or a public acknowledgment on a wall of fame.

The challenge asks for the name of this type of program, written as one word.
